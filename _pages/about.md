      <p>I’m a Lecturer (equivalent to Assistant Professor in the U.S.) in the <a href="https://cis.unimelb.edu.au/research/computer-science/research/software-engineering">School of Computing and Information Systems</a> at <a href="https://cis.unimelb.edu.au/">The University of Melbourne</a>, and an Adjunct Lecturer at <a href="https://research-repository.uwa.edu.au/en/persons/tingting-bi">The University of Western Australia</a>.</p>
